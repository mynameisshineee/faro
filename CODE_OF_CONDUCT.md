# Code of Conduct

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to do good work with incomplete
information, because that is usually true.

Concretely, for this project:

- **Bring evidence.** "This is broken" is a start; the command and its output is a
  report. Disagreements about behaviour are settled by running the thing.
- **Say what you did not check.** A finding with its scope stated is more useful
  than one that sounds complete and is not.
- **Correcting someone is fine. Being unpleasant about it is not.** This repository
  documents several of its authors' own mistakes on purpose; nobody here is
  defending an infallible record.

Harassment, personal attacks, and hostility are not welcome and will get you
removed.

## Scope and reporting

This applies to issues, pull requests, code review and any other space this project
uses.

The intended private channel is the repository's private vulnerability reporting
form (Security → Report a vulnerability): it survives a maintainer changing, and a
conduct report sent through it is read by the maintainers and handled privately.
**It has not been verified as enabled on this repository yet** — see
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md); establishing it is a blocking item before publication.
Do not use an address found in commit metadata: it is a record of who typed
something, not a channel.

Reports are handled by the maintainers listed in [`MAINTAINERS.md`](MAINTAINERS.md).
A maintainer who is the subject of a report does not decide their own case. Outcomes
range from a private word to removal from the project's spaces, and the reporter is
told what was decided.
