## Reaching a maintainer

- **Bugs and features:** open an issue. It is the only channel that leaves a record
  the next person can read.
- **Security:** do not open an issue. Follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
- **Security reports that need a private answer:** the repository's private
  vulnerability reporting form is enabled — see [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
  Do not use an address you find in commit metadata:
  it is a historical record of who typed something, not a support channel.
